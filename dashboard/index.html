THE MATHEMATICS REMAINS

Separate accounting from explanation

For linked papers L, retention c and paired papers R, R = Lc. The linked-paper increase and retention decline reconcile the retained-paper decrease exactly. This accounting does not explain changes in distinct authors.

On a fully allocated paired population, P = A × (I/A) / (I/P) and P/A equals mean fractional credit. With incomplete IDs, the unallocated term must remain explicit.

Δ log R = Δ log L + Δ log c−0.094725 = +0.019192 − 0.113917

Natural-log changes, 2024 to 2025. These are descriptive identities, not causal effects.

WHAT WOULD RESOLVE THIS?

Follow missing bylines through time

Indexing lag is plausible, but this snapshot cannot establish it. We have update dates, not first-indexing dates or historical byline states. Repeated or archived metadata for the same papers is needed to see when IDs were added, replaced or lost.

WHAT THE RESULT MEANS

Growth is observed; its source is unresolved

The manifest contains 3,670 more papers in 2025. That alone does not tell us whether more people contributed.

For this same population, average fractional output per contributor rises if contributor growth is below 7.05%, stays constant if growth is equal, and falls if it is higher.

These are descriptive alternatives, not findings about contributors. Changes in indexing, cross-listing, field composition, and author identity can affect the comparison.

THE DENOMINATOR MATTERS

One contributor, once per period

A contributor is a distinct coauthor on at least one retained paper. Someone with five papers counts once; a paper with three authors counts as one paper and three author–paper incidences. Uploading accounts are not the unit of analysis.

Count both papers and contributors on the same set with complete author identities. In rolling windows, take the union of author IDs across all 12 months.

P = A × (I / A) / (I / P)Papers = contributors × full-count output / mean team size

P/A is mean fractional paper output. I/A is mean full-count publication participation. Neither isolates within-person productivity from changing author composition.

INFERENCE BOUNDARY

What remains unknown

  • How far the observed name/ID comparison generalizes beyond its retained OpenAlex subset.
  • Whether concentration has risen or fallen over a credible historical window.
  • Whether observed new-author entry represents durable participation.
  • Whether any later change can be attributed to AI.

Missingness and population definition are central uncertainties. Conventional sampling p-values cannot resolve them.
